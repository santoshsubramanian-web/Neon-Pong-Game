# Pong

A clean, modern implementation of the classic 1972 arcade game. Simple mechanics, zero fluff, and no microtransactions.

---

## Features

- **Classic Gameplay:** Two paddles, one ball, endless rivalry.
- **Responsive Controls:** Fluid paddle movement optimized for low latency.
- **Score Tracking:** Keeps track of points until someone hits the winning threshold or gives up.
- **Clean Codebase:** Structured to allow easy additions like AI opponents, power-ups, or custom themes.

---

## Controls

| Action | Player 1 (Left) | Player 2 (Right) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Move Up** | `W` | `Up Arrow` |
| **Move Down** | `S` | `Down Arrow` |
| **Pause / Menu** | `P` or `Esc` | `P` or `Esc` |

## Known Issues & Limitations

- The AI opponent (if enabled) is either a physics god or completely blind—there is no middle ground yet.
- Paddles are legally prohibited from moving horizontally. Don't ask.
